01 / PRIVACY

Private, for real

Estimates carry your number plate, VIN and service history. Cloud AI would ship all of it to someone's server. Here, the model runs in your browser — the privacy boundary is the product.

02 / ECONOMICS

$0 marginal cost

Charging $2 a quote only works when each quote costs you nothing. No per-token API bill means the unit economics of this side hustle actually survive contact with reality.

03 / ACCURACY

Niche beats generic

A general chatbot guesses at part prices. WrenchWise pairs a curated fair-price database (the deterministic part) with the open model (the explainer) — each doing what it's actually good at.

Fair ranges are estimates compiled from market data, not quotes — always confirm with a second garage before disputing a bill. WrenchWise is an information tool, not a mechanic. US price pack is in beta: crowdsourced ranges, verify locally.
