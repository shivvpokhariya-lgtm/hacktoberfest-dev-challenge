Three decisions that make this a business instead of a demo.

01

Private by default

Estimates carry your number plate, VIN and service history. Cloud AI would ship all of it to a server. Here the model runs in your browser. The privacy boundary is the product.

02

Zero marginal cost

$2 a quote only works when each quote costs nothing. No tokens, no servers, no per-call bills. The unit economics survive contact with reality.

03

Niche over generic

A chatbot guesses at part prices. A curated price pack does the math; the open model only puts it into words. Each does what it's reliable at.

Fair ranges are estimates compiled from market data, not quotes — always confirm with a second garage before disputing a bill. wrenchwise is an information tool, not a mechanic. US price pack is in beta: crowdsourced ranges, verify locally.
